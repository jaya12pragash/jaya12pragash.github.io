Keeping Distributed AI Inference Reliable Under Overload: Deadline-Aware Backpressure and Adaptive Load Shedding

Presenter: Jayapragash Dakshnamurthy · HealthEquity
Venue: Rochester Institute of Technology (RIT), Rochester, New York
Scheduled slot: 11:00–11:20 a.m. (local time), Saturday, October 24, 2026

DA-BALS uses deadline and congestion signals for admission control and adaptive load shedding in distributed cloud–edge AI inference. The presentation examines how overload can increase queueing delay and missed deadlines, and how deadline-aware backpressure can help maintain useful service under stress.

Research themes: distributed AI inference, cloud–edge systems, deadline-aware scheduling, backpressure, adaptive load shedding, overload resilience.
