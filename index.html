About

I specialize in cloud architecture, distributed systems, microservices modernization, observability, cybersecurity, software reliability, and AI-assisted engineering. My work focuses on designing scalable and dependable platforms, improving production performance, and modernizing complex enterprise systems.

Alongside product engineering, I contribute to the broader engineering and research community through technical publications, peer-reviewed research, conference Program Committee service, and academic reviewing.

My current research interests include reliable AI systems, secure agentic execution, policy-driven runtime governance, failure containment, latency amplification, graph-based anomaly detection, root-cause localization, neural-network reliability, and cyber-resilient distributed architectures.
